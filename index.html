Shuli Chen

I am currently a postdoctoral member at the Institute for Advanced Study, working on geometric analysis.

Previously I was a L. E. Dickson Instrutor at the University of Chicago from January 2025 to August 2026. Before that I was a Uhlenbeck Postdoctoral Fellow at the Simons Laufer Mathematical Sciences Institute (SLMath / MSRI) during fall 2024, attending the program "New Frontiers in Curvature: Flows, General Relativity, Minimal Submanifolds, and Symmetry."

I graduated with a PhD in mathematics from Stanford University in June 2024. My advisor is Otis Chodosh. I obtained my Bachelor of Arts degree from Cornell University in May 2018, and I spent Spring 2017 at the Budapest Semester in Mathematics.

You can reach me by emailing me at shulic AT ias DOT edu.
